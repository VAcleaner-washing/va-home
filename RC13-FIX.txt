VA HOME v13.7.0 RC13 — mobile checkout black strip: root fix

DIAGNOSIS
The strip is exactly #080807 (--color-black, the root background) and exactly
the height reserved for the mobile checkout bar (96px + safe-area-inset-bottom).
.checkout-mobile-bar was position:fixed with backdrop-filter + box-shadow, i.e.
its own hardware compositing layer on iOS. RC9-RC12 tried to hide, transform,
zero out and finally remove that layer from the DOM, while two independent JS
controllers and four competing padding-bottom rules fought over the same state.
When the layer is dropped, iOS leaves the rectangle unpainted (filled with the
root background) and the page reserve stays behind it.

FIX
- .checkout-mobile-bar is now position:sticky in normal flow. No fixed layer.
- No backdrop-filter, no box-shadow, no transform, no will-change on the bar.
- Every body padding-bottom reserve removed (:has(), RC11, RC12) - not needed.
- RC11 iOS viewport-repair block and RC12 detach block deleted from the CSS.
- initIOSKeyboardGuard() (dead code, set transform on <html>) deleted.
- The RC12 detach IIFE deleted. One controller: initMobileCheckoutAction().
- Visibility switch is the native [hidden] attribute only.
- Legacy classes/inline padding cleared on load for returning sessions.
- body.cart-page uses overflow-x:clip so overflow-x:hidden cannot break sticky.
- Asset version bumped to 13.7.0-rc13 (service worker cache invalidation).

TEST
1. Hard reload / remove the PWA from the home screen and re-add it.
2. Cart with 1 item, iOS Safari: scroll down - bar pins above the content,
   disappears when the form enters the viewport, no strip at the bottom.
3. Focus a field, open and close the keyboard, rotate the device.
4. Scroll to the very bottom: footer sits directly under the CTA, no gap.

RC13 ADDENDUM (after the report: also mobile Chrome, only cart, after keyboard)
This confirms the cause is not a WebKit-only compositing quirk. The page was
changing its own layout (body padding-bottom +/- 118px, plus removing a fixed
element from the DOM) at the exact moment the browser was resizing the viewport
for the keyboard. Any engine can leave that newly exposed strip unpainted.
- No layout change at all while a field is focused or the keyboard is open.
- Re-sync happens 300ms after focusout, and only if the keyboard is really gone.

RC13 CLEANUP (technical debt removed, not just overridden)
- .checkout-mobile-bar legacy declarations physically deleted from the mobile
  block: position:fixed, left/right/bottom, z-index:95, backdrop-filter:blur(16px),
  box-shadow:0 -16px 38px. Only flex layout, border-top and background remain.
- Both body:has(.checkout-mobile-bar:not([hidden])) reserves (82px and
  96px + safe-area) deleted from the file.
- All asset version queries synced: 662 references across 51 files are now
  13.7.0-rc13. No page still points at rc11 or rc12.

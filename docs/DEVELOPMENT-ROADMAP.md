# VA HOME — proposed engineering roadmap (not yet approved for implementation)

A. Baseline: verify live version, deployment provenance, environment, tests and PWA. Exit: evidence and blockers.
B. Inventory: architecture, canonical data owners, payment and security, test coverage and CI gaps. Exit: evidence-backed risk register and measured baseline.
C. Engineering process: propose minimal CI and regression gates, release and rollback policy. Exit: approved plan, verified checks and measured feedback time.
D. Product backlog: UX, design system, analytics and growth opportunities; no implementation until separately approved.
Track urgency and impact separately from phase. No new tools, production changes or database writes in audit. Do not assume VAcleaner phase labels apply.
